Vikram holds a BS-MS degree from the Indian Institute of Science Education and Research (IISER), Pune. Prior to joining IISER Mohali for a PhD, he was a Junior Research Fellow at Ashoka University. His PhD research is on understanding hybridization dynamics in bulbuls of the Indian Subcontinent. Apart from this, he is currently leading other collaborative projects in the lab under the broad theme of ecological genomics and community ecology. He is also interested in scientific communication, and documents the flora of india [@thefloraofindia](https://www.instagram.com/thefloraofindia/?hl=en) on Instagram. Apart from these, he is interested in the literary landscapes of the Indian Subcontinent as well. His hobbies include cooking, reading, and working out.
