Atri Bhattacharya completed his B.Sc. in Zoology from Ramakrishna Mission Vivekananda Centenary College (affiliated to WBSU) and his M.Sc. in Biology from Ashoka University, specializing in Behavioral Neuroscience. During his academic training, he worked on multiple research projects across different biological systems, including studies on sleep and infection in Drosophila and decision-making in zebrafish. His master’s thesis examined the effects of acute and chronic stressors on behavior and metabolic physiology in zebrafish, involving behavioral assays, physiological measurements, and statistical data analysis. He has also worked as a research intern in Dr. Poonam Thakur’s laboratory, gaining exposure to molecular and cellular neuroscience techniques, and in Dr. Ajoy Mallick’s laboratory, where he worked on experimental physiology and acquired hands-on training in basic bioinformatics. He is broadly interested in evolutionary biology, genomics, and bioinformatics, and is currently working on epidemiological data analysis and comparative genomic studies of retrotransposon accumulation in birds. Outside of academica, Atri enjoys music, badminton, chess, and occasional reading.
